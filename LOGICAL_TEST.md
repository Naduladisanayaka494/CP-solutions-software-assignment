### Question 1:
**Answer:** **19 days**

#### Explanation:
- **Lily Pad / Exponential Doubling Problem**:
  - *Problem Statement*: A patch of lily pads doubles in size every day. If it takes 20 days for the patch to cover the entire pond, how many days does it take to cover half the pond?
  - *Reasoning*: Because the area doubles every single day, the pond is half-covered on the day immediately preceding full coverage. Therefore, 20 - 1 = **19 days**.

*(Note: In the classic snail climbing problem where a snail climbs 2 meters/feet per day and slips back 1 meter/foot each night in a 20m well, it reaches the top on the **19th day** before slipping).*

### Question 2: Measuring 1 Liter Using a 3L Bottle and a 5L Bottle
**Answer:**
We use the 5L bottle's remaining capacity to transfer 2L from the 3L bottle. Therefore, exactly 1L remains in the 3L bottle.

#### Step-by-Step Procedure:
1. **Step 1**: Fill the **3L bottle** completely to the brim.  
   *(3L bottle: 3L | 5L bottle: 0L)*
2. **Step 2**: Pour the entire contents of the 3L bottle into the **5L bottle**.  
   *(3L bottle: 0L | 5L bottle: 3L)*
3. **Step 3**: Fill the **3L bottle** completely again.  
   *(3L bottle: 3L | 5L bottle: 3L)*
4. **Step 4**: Pour water from the 3L bottle into the 5L bottle until the 5L bottle is completely full.  
   - Since the 5L bottle already has 3L, its remaining capacity is 5L - 3L = **2L**.
   - Only 2L can be transferred from the 3L bottle into the 5L bottle.
5. **Result**:  
   - The 3L bottle now has 3L - 2L = **1L** remaining.  
   *(3L bottle: 1L | 5L bottle: 5L)*
